# Sources for the real-world lessons

Every "In the Philippines (real)" page and the closing guide in `data/lessons.json` states real facts. This file lists where each came from, so a person can re-check it. **Checked on 2026-10-28** by web search; the primary text was opened only where the notes below say so. Anything marked *re-check* should be confirmed by a person (ideally a Filipino teacher, lawyer or journalist) before the game is shared widely.

The made-up stories (Aling Nena, Mark's lola, Mang Berto, Jenny, Lola Fe, reporter Ana) are fiction written to show how each kind of corruption can hurt people; they are labelled as made up in the game.

## Left out on purpose

- Names of living people as wrongdoers. The game names laws, courts, agencies and published events only.
- Court convictions of named people, prison terms, and hotline numbers other than the CSC text line.
- 2026 news (reported new floods, protests and a reported Supreme Court ruling on political dynasties) that the search returned but that could not be confirmed.
